**_Does "Location Not Available" mean that there is something wrong with Honor Magic Vs 2 ?_**

[**_How to distinguish "No Location Found" and "Location Not Available"?_**](https://drfone.wondershare.com/virtual-location/no-location-found-vs-location-not-available.html)

"No Location Found" means that the Find My app is unable to locate the Honor Magic Vs 2 device being tracked. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as if the Honor Magic Vs 2 device is turned off, not connected to the internet, or if location services are disabled.

"Location Not Available" means that the Honor Magic Vs 2 device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Honor Magic Vs 2 device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.
